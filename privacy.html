Privacy Policy

In short (and plain English): we respect your privacy. We collect only the information necessary to provide OrgoHealth’s features, we do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across apps or websites. Your data is used only to make the app work as intended.

This Privacy Policy explains how OrgoHealth (“OrgoHealth”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the OrgoHealth mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name and Email Address
    When you create an account or sign in, we collect your name and email address to identify your account, authenticate you, and provide essential app functionality.
  • Health & Wellness Information
    Information you voluntarily provide when using wellness features, such as:
    • Health-related searches (e.g. holistic remedies, lifestyle or wellness topics)
    • Wellness preferences or inputs used to show relevant content
    This information is used solely to support the app’s core wellness functionality.
  • Search History
    Searches you perform within the app to help you find relevant holistic and wellness-related information.

Information collected automatically

  • Identifiers
    Such as User ID and device identifiers, used to operate the app, maintain sessions, and ensure security.
  • Usage Data
    Including product interactions (such as feature usage and navigation within the app), which helps ensure the app works correctly.
  • Diagnostics Data
    Crash reports and performance data (such as app stability and load times) used to detect issues and improve reliability.
  • Location Information (Precise Location, if enabled)
    Used only when necessary for app functionality. Location data is not linked to your identity.

We do not collect contacts, photos, audio recordings, advertising identifiers, or data unrelated to OrgoHealth’s functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide core app functionality, including account access and wellness features
  • Enable personalized wellness content within the app
  • Maintain app security and prevent misuse
  • Monitor app performance and fix technical issues
  • Improve stability, reliability, and user experience
  • Provide customer support when requested

We do not use your information for third-party advertising, external marketing, or cross-app or cross-website tracking.

3. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

Your information may be shared only in the following limited cases:

  • Service Providers
    With trusted infrastructure or analytics providers strictly for app functionality, diagnostics, and security.
  • Legal Requirements
    If required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

If OrgoHealth is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your information may be stored and processed in countries where OrgoHealth or its service providers operate.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data, including:

  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Restricted access to personal data
  • Monitoring for unauthorized access or misuse

While we strive to protect your information, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

5. Your Privacy Choices

  • You can choose whether to provide certain wellness-related inputs
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable laws
  • You may contact us at any time with privacy-related questions

6. Children’s Privacy

OrgoHealth is not intended for children under the age required by applicable laws. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app or on our website. Continued use of OrgoHealth after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
